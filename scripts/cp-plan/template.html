Rules

  1. Before coding, rewrite the statement in one plain line, then work sample 1 by hand. If you can't do both after 15 minutes, read the Note section and the samples again. Understanding the statement is half the problem right now.
  2. Time-box each problem to 45 minutes. Then read the editorial, close it, and code it yourself.
  3. Ask for help in steps, not for the answer. From Claude or the editorial, take one hint at a time: first "which topic is this?", then "what is the key observation?", and only then the solution. Stop reading as soon as you can continue alone.
  4. Tap "needed help" on any problem where you used a hint, the editorial or Claude. It comes back the next day as a re-solve from a blank file. That re-solve is where the learning happens.
  5. Before you submit, test n = 1, all values equal, the maximum values, and overflow (use long long).
  6. Second WA on the same problem? Stop guessing. Write the brute force and run stress/stress.sh from your CP folder. Fix the failing case it prints, then resubmit.
  7. Rounds start at 20:05, right after your meeting. Do every one live. If the meeting runs over, register anyway and start late. Skip the round only if work is on fire, then do it virtual in the next 10:45 block.
  8. CSES stuck past 45 minutes: skip it. You're missing a technique. Come back after the next topic.
  9. Write one line per missed idea in the day's log box.
  10. LeetCode daily is a 30-minute cap. It keeps interview patterns warm; it does not move your rating. If a Hard runs past 30 minutes, read the editorial and move on.
  11. Missed a day? Don't catch up. Do today's plan and move on. If the day was a holiday or you were ill, open it and press "Mark as off day": that day's study plan moves to the next day of the same kind (weekday or weekend) and everything after it shifts by one, the day drops out of the totals, and the streak skips it. Undo pulls the plan back. Be honest with it; it's for days you couldn't, not days you didn't.
  12. LeetCode contests come in at stage 1: Weekly on Sunday 08:00 IST, Biweekly on Saturday 20:00 IST. Do Weekly when there is no Codeforces round the night before; skip Biweekly if it clashes with a round.
